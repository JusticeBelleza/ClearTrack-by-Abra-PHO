# Full Privacy Policy for FileTrackr

**1. Introduction**
The Abra Provincial Health Office (PHO) is committed to protecting the personal data of its employees and authorized personnel. This Privacy Policy explains how the FileTrackr Document Tracker collects, processes, and protects your information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
*   **Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

**2. Who is Responsible for Your Personal Data**
*   **Organization:** Abra Provincial Health Office
*   **Address:** OLD APH Compound Calaba, Bangued, Abra, Philippines
*   **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** Antonio L. Valera, M.D.
*   **System Administration:** System oversight is managed by the ICT Officer Designate, Justice P. Belleza. Inquiries regarding system data can be directed to the ICT office.

**3. Personal Information We Collect**
As an internal administrative tool, FileTrackr collects only the data strictly necessary for system operation:
*   **Account Information:** Full name, official government email address, department assignment, and designated system role.
*   **Activity & Audit Logs:** Timestamps of logins, documents routed, actions executed (e.g., received, forwarded), and system usage metrics.
*   **IP/Device Information:** IP addresses, browser types, and device identifiers collected during active sessions to detect and prevent unauthorized access.
*   **Cookies & Security Tokens:** Cryptographic tokens generated by security verification widgets.
*   *(Note: FileTrackr supports biometric login via passkeys, but biometric templates—such as Face ID or fingerprints—are processed and stored exclusively on your local device. The system only collects the cryptographic approval, never your sensitive physical biometric data.)*

**4. How and Why We Use Personal Information**
We process your personal information for the following purposes:
*   **Accountability & Tracking:** To maintain a transparent, verifiable chain of custody for all official documents. *(Lawful Basis: Public authority functions and legitimate organizational interests)*.
*   **Security & Access Control:** To authenticate users, enforce role-based access restrictions via Row Level Security (RLS), and block automated bot attacks. *(Lawful Basis: Legitimate interests / Protection of vital system infrastructure)*.

**5. Disclosure and Sharing of Personal Information**
Your data is strictly confidential and is only disclosed to the following entities:
*   **Authorized Personnel:** System administrators, your direct supervisors, and specific colleagues you interact with during document routing.
*   **Hosting & Cloud Providers:** Our cloud database provider (Supabase) and network security provider (Cloudflare), strictly for hosting and securing the application infrastructure.
*   **Government Authorities:** The Commission on Audit (COA), Civil Service Commission (CSC), or other legal authorities, but only when formally and legally required for compliance or auditing purposes.
*   *We do not sell, rent, or share your data with unauthorized third parties or commercial business partners.*

**6. Data Retention**
Personal information and audit logs are retained in accordance with the official records disposition schedules mandated by the National Archives of the Philippines (NAP) and local provincial guidelines. Upon separation from the agency, your active login access will be revoked immediately; however, historical routing logs associated with your name must remain intact indefinitely to preserve the integrity of official government records.

**7. Security Measures**
We implement robust organizational, physical, and technical safeguards, including strict database Row Level Security (RLS) policies, encrypted data transmissions, and bot protection challenges. While we continuously monitor the system for vulnerabilities, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

**8. Cookies and Similar Technologies**
FileTrackr does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We utilize strictly necessary technologies, such as Cloudflare Turnstile, which issues temporary security tokens to verify that you are a legitimate human user and not an automated script.

**9. International Transfers**
To ensure high availability and robust security, FileTrackr’s primary database infrastructure is hosted on cloud servers located in the AWS `ap-northeast-2` region (Seoul, South Korea). Data transferred to these servers is encrypted at rest and in transit, governed by strict enterprise data processing agreements that align with the DPA.

**10. Rights of Data Subjects**
Under the Data Privacy Act, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data in FileTrackr:
*   **Right to be Informed:** To know what data is collected and how it is used.
*   **Right to Access:** To view your profile and personal audit history.
*   **Right to Correction/Rectification:** To request updates to incorrect department or role assignments.
*   **Right to Object:** To object to unauthorized data processing.
*   **Right to Erasure or Blocking:** To request deletion of data (Note: this right is limited where data retention is legally required for official government audit trails).
*   **Right to Data Portability:** To obtain a copy of your data in a secure format.
*   **Right to File a Complaint:** With the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
*   **Right to Damages:** For privacy violations.

**11. How to Exercise Privacy Rights**
To exercise any of the above rights, submit a written request via official email to the ICT Officer Designate. Identity verification (e.g., presenting your official PHO ID) will be required. Requests will generally be processed within 15 working days.

**12. Data Breach Policy**
In the event of a security incident compromising personal data, the ICT office will immediately contain the breach, notify affected personnel, and report the incident to the National Privacy Commission (NPC) within 72 hours, as required by law.

**13. Children's Privacy**
FileTrackr is an internal enterprise tool restricted to employed, adult personnel. It does not intentionally collect information from minors.

**14. Changes to the Privacy Policy**
We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via an administrative announcement on the FileTrackr dashboard upon your next login.

**15. Contact / Complaints**
For any concerns regarding this policy or the handling of your data, please contact the ICT Officer Designate or the Data Protection Officer directly.
